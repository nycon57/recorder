Handling refund disputes and chargebacks. When a customer goes to their bank instead of coming to us, we get a chargeback notification via Stripe. This is different from a refund request — we have to respond to the bank with evidence, and we have a deadline. Stripe sends an email and creates an alert in the disputes panel. First action: check if the customer also submitted a support ticket. If yes, link the chargeback to the ticket. Sometimes it's the same issue — they got frustrated waiting and went to their bank. In that case, if the refund is legitimate, issue it directly and submit evidence to Stripe showing the refund was processed. This usually gets the chargeback reversed without a fee. If the chargeback is not legitimate — fraud case or not-as-described but we delivered the service — we respond with evidence: the signed contract or terms acceptance, delivery confirmation, any customer communication showing they used the product. Evidence submission is done in Stripe's dispute panel. Deadline is typically 7 days. Missing the deadline means automatic loss of the chargeback. Priya owns all chargeback responses over $500. Under $500, you can respond directly but loop her in.
