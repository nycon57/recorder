Zendesk CSAT survey configuration and response handling. CSAT surveys go out automatically 24 hours after a ticket is marked solved. The trigger is in Automations — do not disable it or change the 24-hour window without talking to Priya first, because it affects how we benchmark against industry standards. When a customer submits a bad CSAT, it creates a follow-up task in the CSAT review queue. Agents are expected to review every 1 or 2 rating they receive and add an internal note explaining what happened and what they'd do differently. This is not punitive — it's how we build the training content. The three most common causes of bad CSAT in our data: first, response time exceeded SLA. Second, issue wasn't resolved in first contact. Third, the customer felt the agent didn't understand their problem. For the third category, re-reading your ticket thread critically helps. If you wrote more than two paragraphs asking clarifying questions before you actually addressed the problem, that's likely why the customer felt unheard. The goal is to acknowledge the problem first, ask questions second.
