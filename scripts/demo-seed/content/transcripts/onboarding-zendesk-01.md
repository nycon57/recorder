Today we're walking through onboarding a new tier-1 support agent into Zendesk. Start at Settings, then People, then Team members. Click Add team member, drop them into the Tier 1 group, and assign the Agent role — not Light agent, which restricts ticket commenting. Make sure the default ticket view is Unsolved tickets in my groups so they see work the moment they log in. One thing folks miss — go to Views and pin Your unsolved tickets and Recently updated to their sidebar. Without that, they spend week one hunting for work. For macros, we start them with the three tier-1 canned responses: greeting-initial, info-requested, and closing-resolved. Have them send a test ticket to themselves on day one — I cannot overstate how much that surfaces permission gaps early. Finally, the Talk voice integration needs explicit enable per-agent; new hires will not get calls routed to them if you skip that toggle. Whole process takes about twelve minutes per agent. Document everything you do in the agent's onboarding checklist in Confluence so there's an audit trail when HR asks.
