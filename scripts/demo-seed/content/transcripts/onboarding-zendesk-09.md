Using Zendesk Explore for personal performance dashboards. Every agent should check their own metrics weekly — not just wait for Priya to send the monthly report. In Explore, go to Dashboards and you'll find the Agent Performance template I set up last quarter. It shows first reply time, resolution time, CSAT score, and tickets solved per day for whatever date range you pick. Filter by your own name in the Assignee field. The most useful metric for self-coaching is the distribution of your first reply times. If 80% of your replies are within 2 hours but 20% are over 8 hours, those outliers are where you want to focus. Usually it means something in your workflow is causing you to lose track of certain tickets. The second metric I look at is CSAT comments, not just scores. A 4 out of 5 with a comment saying the issue took too long to resolve tells you something different than a 4 with no comment. Both are the same score but very different situations. Export your CSAT comments to a spreadsheet monthly and look for patterns in the negative feedback.
