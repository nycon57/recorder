Zendesk ticket tagging conventions — this matters for our reporting. Every ticket should have at least one issue-type tag and one product-area tag. Issue types we use: billing-question, refund-request, technical-issue, account-access, feature-request, onboarding-help. Product areas: payments, integrations, reporting, api, notifications. Tags are case-sensitive in Zendesk — billing-question and Billing-Question are two different tags. I've seen agents add typo tags that pollute our reports for months. The way to prevent this: go to Business rules, then Tags, and enable the tag allowlist. Only pre-approved tags can be added. Right now there are 34 approved tags. If a new agent needs a tag that's not on the list, they request it through Priya. Takes about a day to get approved and added. Second thing: some tags trigger automations. The tag escalate-to-engineering fires a webhook that creates a Jira ticket automatically. Don't apply that tag unless the issue actually needs engineering — we've had false positives that wasted engineering sprint time.
