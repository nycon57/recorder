Subscription cancellation refunds — slightly different flow than one-time payment refunds. When a customer cancels a subscription and requests a refund of the unused portion, we use the proration formula: days remaining divided by total billing days times the subscription amount. Example: customer paid $120 for a 30-day period, cancels on day 10. They have 20 days remaining. Refund is 20 divided by 30 times $120 equals $80. In HubSpot, find the subscription under the company record, not the deal record. Subscriptions live in a separate panel called Subscriptions. Set the cancellation date and check the proration checkbox — it calculates the amount automatically if the Stripe integration is working. If it shows zero or blank, calculate manually and enter it. Tag the ticket subscription-cancellation-refund. These are tracked separately from deal refunds in our reporting. One important thing: if the customer is on an annual plan and cancels before the halfway mark, they're entitled to a prorated refund per our terms. After the halfway mark it's goodwill-only. Check the contract date, not the invoice date.
