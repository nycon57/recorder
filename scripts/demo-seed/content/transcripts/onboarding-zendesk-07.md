How we handle ticket merging in Zendesk. When a customer submits the same issue multiple times — which happens constantly when people don't see an auto-reply — we merge duplicates. The rule: always keep the ticket that has the most context or the longest conversation history. Merge the others into it. To merge: open the ticket you want to keep, click the gear icon, select Merge, and search for the duplicate by ticket number or customer email. The merged ticket's comments get appended to the primary ticket. The merged ticket gets closed with a note pointing to the primary. Important: the customer gets an email notification when their ticket is merged. The notification says your ticket has been merged into ticket number X. This sometimes causes confusion — customers think their issue is being dismissed. So after merging, always add a personal public comment on the primary ticket acknowledging the situation. Something like I've combined your two tickets so we can keep everything in one place. I'm reviewing it now. This has reduced merge-related complaints by about 60% since we started doing it.
