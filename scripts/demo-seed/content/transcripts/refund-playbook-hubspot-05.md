Handling goodwill refunds — these are different from standard refunds because there's no billing error, just a customer retention decision. Goodwill refunds are capped at $200 per customer per rolling 12 months without additional approval. Above that cap, you need Priya's sign-off. To process a goodwill refund: open the deal, set Refund reason to goodwill, Refund status to Partial requested or Full requested depending on amount. In the activity log, add a note explaining the customer situation and why you're recommending goodwill. Be specific — not the customer was upset but the customer has been with us for 3 years and this is their first billing issue, so retaining them outweighs the $75 refund cost. Finance audits goodwill refunds quarterly to check if we're using them strategically or just to make complaints go away. The ratio we aim for is goodwill refunds should be under 15% of all refunds by volume. We were at 22% last quarter which prompted a review. If you're unsure whether a situation warrants goodwill, ask in the support-billing Slack channel before processing.
