Monthly refund report preparation for the Finance team. This runs on the last business day of the month. Pull the Stripe refunds export: Payments, then Refunds, then set date range to the full month, export CSV. The CSV has payment ID, amount, currency, reason code, and created date. Next, open HubSpot and run the custom report Refunds by Month from the Finance folder in Reports. This report joins on Stripe Payment Intent and gives you deal owner, deal stage, company name, and the HubSpot refund reason alongside the Stripe data. The two reports won't always match perfectly — there's usually a 2 to 5 percent mismatch rate from webhook delays or manual entries. For any refund in Stripe not in HubSpot, add a row to the reconciliation tab of the shared Finance spreadsheet with the payment ID, amount, date, and a note saying webhook-gap or manual-backfill. Finance needs the reconciliation tab completed by noon on the last business day. If you're going to miss the deadline, tell Priya by 10am so she can finish it. The CFO reviews this report in the quarterly board deck.
