Integration health monitoring — how we know when the Jira-Zendesk connection breaks. The integration drops about once a quarter, usually after a Zendesk or Jira version update. Symptoms: the Jira panel in Zendesk shows a spinner indefinitely, or creates a Jira issue but no backlink appears on the Zendesk ticket within 5 minutes. If you see either of these, check the Zendesk app health in Admin Center: Apps and integrations, then Jira, then View health status. If there's a red status, flag it in the tech-ops Slack channel immediately — don't spend time troubleshooting it yourself. The fix is usually a re-authentication of the service account. We have a dedicated service account for the integration, not an individual user account. This is why agents shouldn't use personal Jira credentials in the integration config. If the service account password rotates or the account gets flagged, the whole integration drops. Last time this happened it took 4 hours to diagnose because nobody realized it was the service account. Priya owns the service account and gets alerted when it's flagged.
