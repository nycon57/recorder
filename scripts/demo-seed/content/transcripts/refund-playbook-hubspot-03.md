Full refund process for post-close deals in HubSpot. This is different from partial — full refunds require a separate approval chain. First, confirm the refund basis with the customer in writing — email or ticket comment, not a call note. Get them to state clearly what they're requesting a full refund for. This protects us if the refund is ever disputed. Second, open the deal in HubSpot and set Refund status to Full requested. Create a Billing ticket if there isn't one, mark it High priority, and tag it refund-full. Third step is the approval — full refunds need sign-off from Priya as admin and from the deal's account owner. Add a task in HubSpot assigned to both of them. Do not proceed to Stripe until both tasks are marked complete. Fourth, process the refund in Stripe via the deal sidebar. If the original charge is more than 90 days old, Stripe can't reverse it — you'll have to issue a manual ACH credit instead. That goes through Finance, not through Stripe directly. Fifth, update the deal stage in HubSpot to Refunded and close the Billing ticket as resolved.
