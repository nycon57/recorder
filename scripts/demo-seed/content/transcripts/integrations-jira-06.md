Weekly sync between support and engineering — how it works and why each team gets what they need out of it. We meet Tuesdays at 10am. Engineering brings their sprint board; support brings the top 10 customer-impacting Jira issues by ticket-link count. The first 15 minutes is status updates: which issues moved to Done last week, which ones need more information from support. If an engineering team needs customer reproduction steps or additional logs for a bug, they assign a sub-task to the Zendesk ticket reporter. We have 48 hours to get that information. If we can't get it from the customer in 48 hours, the Jira issue goes to On Hold and we document why. The second 15 minutes is new escalations: anything that came in since last Tuesday that's sev-1 or sev-2. Engineering lead does a quick triage and assigns to a sprint or backlog. The last 15 minutes is process improvement — one item from support, one from engineering. Last month we used that time to fix the label scheme so sev-4 issues stopped being mislabeled as sev-2. Reducing that noise cut engineering triage time by about 30 minutes per sprint.
