End-of-shift handoff procedures for support agents. Before you sign off, three things have to happen. First, clear your pending queue. Any ticket waiting on customer response that's been sitting more than 48 hours should get a follow-up nudge. Use the macro Follow-up after no response. If they don't respond within 72 hours of that, you can mark it solved. Second, check your SLA breach risk view and handle anything that will breach within 4 hours of your shift ending. If you can't resolve it in time, escalate to whoever's on next shift with a handoff note. Third, internal notes on anything unresolved. If I pick up a ticket from Marcus in the morning with no context, I'm starting from zero. A two-sentence internal note saying what you've tried and what the customer last said saves ten minutes of re-reading the ticket thread. We use Slack channel support-handoffs for anything urgent that needs a live heads-up, not just a Zendesk note. If a customer said they're going to escalate to their VP if this isn't resolved by tomorrow morning, that goes in Slack.
