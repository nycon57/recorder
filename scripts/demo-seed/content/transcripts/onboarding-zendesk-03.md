Quick walkthrough of Zendesk views configuration for new agents. Default views are basically useless out of the box — they show everything to everyone which is overwhelming. For a new tier-1 agent, I create three pinned views: My open tickets, SLA breach risk in next 4 hours, and Waiting for customer. The fourth view I add is Group: Tier 1 unassigned — this is the work queue they pull from when their own queue is empty. Filters on that view: status open or pending, assignee is nobody, group is Tier 1. Sort by created ascending so the oldest ticket gets picked first. Do not sort by priority — that creates cherry-picking behavior where agents only grab easy tickets. One thing that trips people up: pending status means you're waiting on the customer. If an agent sets a ticket pending and the customer responds, Zendesk auto-reopens it and it resurfaces in the My open tickets view. Walk them through this on day two or they'll wonder why tickets they thought were closed keep coming back.
