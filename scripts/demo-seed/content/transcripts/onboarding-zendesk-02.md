Zendesk business hours and SLAs — this is the piece most new admins underthink. Go to Settings, then Objects and rules, then Business rules, then Business hours. Create one schedule per region we support; right now that's US-East, US-West, and EU-Central. Put timezone and holiday calendar in. Next, SLAs. The one people miss: first-reply-time SLA needs to be scoped to the Agent responded event, not Ticket created. Creating an SLA against ticket-created breaks the moment you add automation that auto-assigns on weekends — the clock starts during off-hours. For priority Urgent, first reply is 30 minutes, resolution is 4 hours. For High, it's 2 hours and 24 hours. Medium and Low don't have a resolution SLA — we track them, we don't commit. Tier-1 agents should see their SLA breach risk in the sidebar widget. If they don't, the widget needs enabling per-view: Views, then the view name, then Show SLA column. Check this on every new agent's first login.
