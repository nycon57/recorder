Using Jira automation to keep customers updated on bug status. We have three automations configured in Jira that fire notifications back to Zendesk when engineering takes action. First: when a Jira issue moves to In Progress, the linked Zendesk tickets get an internal note saying Engineering has started working on this issue. Agents then send a quick customer update. Second: when a Jira issue moves to Done and resolution is Fixed, it fires a webhook to Zendesk that adds the tag jira-fix-deployed to all linked tickets. Agents have a saved view for tickets with that tag — their queue of tickets to close with confirmation. Third: when a Jira issue is closed as Won't Fix or Duplicate, agents get notified so they can manage customer expectations. The notification includes the engineering team's comment explaining why. This is important — you can't tell a customer their bug won't be fixed without giving them some context. The engineering comment is your starting point. Sometimes it's straightforward: this behavior is by design. Sometimes it needs more diplomacy. That's part of the job.
