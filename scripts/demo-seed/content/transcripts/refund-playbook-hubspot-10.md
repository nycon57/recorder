Preventing refunds through proactive billing communication. The best refund is the one that never happens. These are the three interventions we've found most effective. First, pre-billing notification — we send an email 7 days before any charge over $200. This gives customers a chance to flag issues before the charge hits their card. This alone reduced refund requests by 18% when we implemented it last year. Second, failed payment recovery. When a payment fails, we have a 3-email sequence over 5 days before we suspend the account. Most failed payments are card expiry or insufficient funds — both fixable if we reach out quickly. Don't wait for the customer to notice the service interruption. Third, usage-based check-in for annual customers at the 6-month mark. If they're not using the product they're paying for, they'll churn and request a refund at renewal. A proactive call at 6 months to review adoption catches this before it becomes a refund conversation. These three practices together reduced our refund rate from 8.2% to 5.4% over 18 months.
