Reconciliation — this runs weekly on Thursdays. We pull the Stripe refund report for the last 7 days, export CSV, and match against HubSpot deals. The match key is the payment intent ID — it's in HubSpot as a custom property called Stripe Payment Intent. If the Payment Intent field is blank on a deal, the Stripe webhook missed it and you have to backfill manually by looking at the deal's Close Date and matching on customer email in Stripe. For any refund that doesn't match a HubSpot deal, check if it's a subscription cancellation — those don't touch deals, they come off the customer record directly. Create a HubSpot note on the customer record documenting the refund reason and amount. Reason codes we use: duplicate-charge, not-as-described, cancelled-trial-charge, fraud-suspected, goodwill. Anything labeled fraud-suspected also pings Priya via the shared Slack channel — she owns the quarterly Stripe dispute review.
