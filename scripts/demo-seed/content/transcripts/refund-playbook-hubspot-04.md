HubSpot deal properties we use for refund tracking — making sure everyone has these configured correctly on their deals view. Go to your Deals board, click the settings gear, then Manage properties. The required properties for any deal that involves billing: Refund status, Stripe Payment Intent, Refund amount, Refund reason, Refund approved by, Refund date. If these don't show up in your property options, Priya needs to add them to the deal view for your pipeline. Don't add them yourself — we have a specific order they go in and custom field rules that govern who can edit each one. The Refund approved by field is locked to admin users only. Agents see it as read-only. This prevents agents from approving their own refund requests, which happened once before we added the restriction. The Refund date field auto-populates from the Stripe webhook when the refund is processed — you shouldn't need to set it manually. If it's blank after you've processed the refund in Stripe, the webhook connection may have dropped. Check the HubSpot integration health dashboard.
