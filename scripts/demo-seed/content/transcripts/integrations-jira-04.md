Jira triage labels and how engineering uses them to prioritize support escalations. When you create a Jira issue from Zendesk, the mandatory labels are: customer-impacting, the product-area label, and the severity label. Product area labels: payments-integration, notifications, api-access, reporting-module, account-management. Severity labels: sev-1-data-loss, sev-2-feature-broken, sev-3-degraded, sev-4-cosmetic. Sev-1 means a customer has lost data or cannot access their account at all. This goes straight to the engineering Slack channel immediately — do not just create a Jira issue and wait. Sev-2 means a core feature is completely broken for the customer. Engineering reviews these in the daily standup. Sev-3 is degraded functionality — the feature works but poorly. These get scheduled into the next sprint. Sev-4 is cosmetic issues like display glitches. These go into the backlog. Mislabeling severity is one of the things that causes friction between support and engineering. If I mark something sev-1 and it's actually sev-3, engineering loses trust in our escalations. When in doubt, go one level lower than you think — it's easier to upgrade severity than to defend over-escalation.
