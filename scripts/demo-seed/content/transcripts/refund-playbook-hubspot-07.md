Duplicate charge refunds — these are the simplest refunds but also the most time-sensitive because customers notice them immediately. When a customer reports a duplicate charge, first verify in Stripe by searching their email. Look at the payment timeline. If you see two charges for the same amount within 24 hours to the same customer, it's almost certainly a duplicate. Before issuing the refund, check HubSpot to see if there are two deals closed for the same product in the same period. Sometimes what looks like a duplicate is actually a legitimate second purchase. If it's confirmed duplicate — refund the newer charge, not the older one. Keep the original because that's the one tied to their account activation or subscription. In Stripe, find the duplicate payment, click Refund, full amount, reason is duplicate. The refund reason flows back to HubSpot via webhook and tags the deal automatically with tag duplicate-charge. These should be processed same-day — don't let duplicate charge reports sit. Customer anger over duplicate charges escalates faster than almost any other billing issue.
