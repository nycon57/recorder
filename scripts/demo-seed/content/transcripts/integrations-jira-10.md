Quarterly Jira audit — cleaning up stale issues in the SUP project. Every quarter we review all open Jira issues older than 60 days. The audit has three steps. Step one: identify issues where the linked Zendesk tickets are all closed. If the customers' tickets are all resolved but the Jira issue is still open, either the bug was worked around or it was never actually a bug. Reach out to the engineering assignee and ask if it's still relevant. If nobody responds in a week, close the Jira issue as Won't Fix with a note explaining the linked Zendesk tickets were resolved. Step two: issues where the customer count is zero or null. These are usually internal reports that slipped into the SUP project. Move them to the appropriate engineering project. Step three: On Hold issues older than 90 days. These are bugs where we couldn't get customer reproduction steps. After 90 days, it's unlikely we're going to get them. Close as Cannot Reproduce and leave a comment saying we attempted to gather reproduction steps and were unsuccessful. The quarterly audit keeps the project board clean and engineering focused on real customer problems. We went from 340 open issues to 87 after the last audit.
