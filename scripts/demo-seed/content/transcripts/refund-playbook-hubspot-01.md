This is the partial-refund playbook for HubSpot deals that came back post-close. Open the deal record — it should be in Closed Won — and find the Refund status custom property under the Finance section. Set it to Partial requested. In the Activity tab, log a call or email with the customer's refund rationale; never rely on memory. Next, open the associated ticket. If there's no ticket, create one: type Billing, priority High, link to the deal. Add the refund line item to the ticket as a structured field, not as a note. Finance pulls from structured fields weekly — notes get lost. For amounts over $500, you need at-mentions on Priya and the deal owner before submitting to Stripe. We've had two cases this quarter where tier-1 agents issued refunds without dual approval and we had to claw back via Stripe disputes. Don't be that person. Once approved, Stripe refund goes through the Stripe integration panel on the deal sidebar — not the Stripe dashboard directly. Integration keeps HubSpot in sync.
