Atlassian Rovo integration setup and how it helps with support workflows. Rovo is Atlassian's AI layer that sits across Jira and Confluence. For support teams, the most useful feature is its ability to search Confluence documentation from inside a Zendesk ticket. When you're on a ticket and you need to find the relevant KB article or internal procedure, instead of switching to Confluence and searching manually, you can use the Rovo assistant in the Jira panel sidebar. Type your question in natural language and it searches Confluence and returns the relevant sections. This saved our team about 25 minutes per agent per day when we piloted it last quarter. Setup is per-agent: in the Jira panel, there's a Rovo tab. They'll need to authenticate with their Atlassian account, same SSO. Once authenticated, queries automatically scope to our workspace — they won't see other companies' documentation. One caveat: Rovo searches Confluence, not our Zendesk macros or internal notes. For macro search, still use the Zendesk native macro search. We're working on connecting Rovo to our Tribora knowledge base too, which would give it access to our recorded training content.
