# Customer Communication Guidelines

**Owner:** Priya Rangan
**Applies to:** All support channels (email, chat, voice)

## Core Principles

1. **Acknowledge before asking** — State you understand the problem before requesting more information.
2. **One question per message** — Multiple questions in one reply increase the time to resolution. Prioritize your most important question.
3. **Avoid jargon** — Write for a non-technical audience unless you have confirmed they are technical.
4. **Time commitments are binding** — If you say "I'll have an answer for you by end of day," that's a commitment. Miss it and you must proactively explain why.

## Tone Standards

- Empathetic but efficient — acknowledge frustration, don't dwell on it
- Professional but not corporate — write like a knowledgeable human, not a legal document
- Action-oriented — lead with what you're doing, not what you cannot do

## What to Avoid

- "Unfortunately" as a sentence opener — leads with negativity
- "I understand how frustrating this must be" as a rote opener — sounds scripted
- Passive voice on ownership — say "I'll check this" not "this will be looked into"
- Asking for information already in the ticket

## Response Time Commitments

When you commit to a follow-up, add a task in Zendesk with a due date. The task ensures the system reminds you — don't rely on memory.

## Escalation Language

When escalating: "I'm bringing in our senior team to get you the best answer on this" — not "I don't know" or "this isn't something I can help with."
